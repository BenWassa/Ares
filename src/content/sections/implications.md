## Part V — Implications, Prevention & Research Boundaries

### What the current synthesis can support

The source-reviewed Part IV synthesis supports a restrained conclusion: extreme mass homicide cannot be explained by a single perpetrator type or a universal causal sequence. Political and historical conditions, construction of target groups, authorization and organization, situational pressures, group norms and individual variation can interact in materially different ways across cases.

That explanatory frame can help readers formulate better questions about institutions, rhetoric, command structures, participation and refusal. It does **not** by itself validate a prediction score, a fixed set of warning stages, or a claim that observing one domain means another will follow.

### Prevention claims require their own evidence

Earlier Ares drafts converted the legacy process ladder directly into an intervention checklist. Issue #10 rejected that move because the prevention claims had not been independently source-mapped. Ares 2.0 therefore does not present the Part IV synthesis as an operational early-warning instrument.

Policies involving hate-speech monitoring, arms control, judicial intervention, sanctions, peacekeeping, accountability mechanisms or clinical risk assessment each have separate evidence bases, legal constraints and potential harms. They should not inherit authority from a diagram simply because they sound plausible. A future prevention section should add those claims only with explicit sources and scope conditions.

### Research directions

The synthesis instead exposes questions suitable for further study: how different domains interact across case types; which institutional conditions change participation or refusal; how propaganda and perceived threat interact with organizational authorization; and where massacre and genocide require distinct explanatory models. Comparative and longitudinal research can test those relationships without assuming the four-domain Ares synthesis is itself a validated predictive model.

### Release boundary

This release prioritizes faithful explanation, provenance and transparent uncertainty. It deliberately leaves an operational prevention framework out of scope until its claims can be researched and sourced independently.
